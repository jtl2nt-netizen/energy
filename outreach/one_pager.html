What it means for an installer or retailer

  • The value is real where exports are paid at market prices and largest in homes with an EV. Homes on a fixed export price gain little from battery scheduling, so we would not sell it to them.
  • Most installed batteries run on default settings today. The saving can be passed to customers, sold as a monthly service, or used to win new sales against utility offers.
  • No hardware. The engine works through the inverter and charger interfaces you already use, and can run read-only first.

Proposed next step

A free, read-only analysis on 50–100 of your installations over a few weeks. We compute what the controller would have done; nothing is switched. You receive a per-home report of the saving before deciding on a pilot.

Method: one home per city simulated hour by hour through 2025: 5 kWp solar (30° south), 10 kWh / 5 kW battery, 3,500 kWh/yr household load, EV of 2,350 kWh/yr with a weekday commute, 3.7 kW charger, 6.9 kVA contract. Prices: OMIE day-ahead 2025 (PT and ES zones). Tariffs: ERSE 2026 regulated BTN prices with VAT (PT); 2026 2.0TD network charges and cargos with taxes (ES). Export at the hourly market price minus €10/MWh, or fixed. Solar: NASA POWER 2025 radiation. Forecasts use only data available at decision time. "Smart EV charger" follows solar surplus and the cheapest known hours. Not modelled: Spain's monthly compensation cap, 15-minute settlement. Results for a real fleet will differ; the read-only analysis exists to measure them.
