# Iberian prospect list: white-label home-energy optimisation engine (October 2026)

**Product being pitched:** a licensed engine that decides, continuously, for homes with rooftop PV + home battery (often + EV charger) when to self-consume, charge the battery, charge the EV, import or export, using price and solar/load forecasts.

**Ideal buyer:** sells/installs PV+battery (or the control hardware) in Portugal or Spain, has thousands of installed homes, and shows no strong in-house optimisation/ML capability.

**Method and conventions (read before using):**
- Research date 2026-10-12. Each claim carries its source and the source's date. "Claim" marks company marketing or self-reported figures. "Inference" marks my own reading.
- Websites were checked with an HTTP request on 2026-10-12. "200" means the page loaded. "403 (resolves)" means the domain answers but blocks automated requests, which is normal for large utilities. No site was left unchecked.
- Contact channels are only ones published on the company's own site (generic addresses or form URLs). No personal emails or names were guessed. Where no generic address is published, the form or contact page is given.
- Categories: **buyer** (would license), **channel partner** (could resell or bundle the engine to many installers/homes), **competitor** (already sells or runs this kind of optimisation).
- Priority: **A** = pursue first, **B** = pursue after A or needs a trigger, **C** = low fit or low evidence.

## 2. Short notes per prospect (pitch facts)

**EDP Comercial (PT).** It has the largest residential PV base in Portugal (>140k homes per the Apr 2025 claim). Its hourly OMIE-indexed "Oferta Indexada" means that time-shifting with a battery pays off directly. Battery and inverter brands were not public: the EDP site blocks automated reading and customer complaints (2026) mention "incompatible" batteries, which suggests a mixed fleet (inference). Ask whether EDP already controls batteries. If it does, it is likely the "PT in-house" reference case; then treat it as a competitor and use it as proof in the pitch.

**Galp Solar (PT/ES).** In 2025 Galp attached roughly 1 battery to every 6–7 new installs (~250 batteries over ~1,600–1,700 installs; RIG 2025). It is pushing attach rates with price incentives: €800 off a battery kit for Galp electricity customers, and €200 off via DECO PROteste since Aug 2026 ([DECO](https://www.deco.proteste.pt/vantagens-subscritores/artigos/paineis-solares-com-desconto-galp)). Galp Casa is listed among Portuguese retailers with dynamic/indexed tariffs ([comparamais, undated](https://www.comparamais.pt/luz-e-gas/tarifas/tipos-de-tarifas/variaveis/)). Pitch angle: lift battery attach and cut battery-related complaints with automated, price-aware control.

**Goldenergy (PT).** All-GoodWe fleet, so the GoodWe SEMS/SEMS+ cloud API is the only integration needed. It buys surplus at 15-min OMIE (0% fee) or 0.025 €/kWh fixed, and requires its customers to be Goldenergy supply customers. That makes it both the retailer and the off-taker, so it captures the arbitrage value. It also sells Wallbox chargers.

**Otovo (PT/ES).** Leasing customers (0 € upfront, 20-year contracts) leave the asset on Otovo's books. Its HEMS today is Huawei EMMA, so the pitch is to make the fleet brand-agnostic, which matters because Otovo uses many subcontracted installers.

**SotySolar (ES/PT).** Residential only, Marguerite-backed (infrastructure investors value recurring software or flex revenue). It sells batteries and EV chargers. Its brand mix is unknown because the site blocks automated reading.

**Iberdrola, Contigo.** Iberdrola's Solar Cloud and community model is credit-based rather than control-based. The Contigo acquisition (signed 28 Sep 2026) creates a separately run brand of >100k customers with its own PV install business, which is a natural pilot sandbox once the deal closes.

**Endesa, Naturgy, Repsol/Solar360, TotalEnergies (ES).** All run free or cheap virtual batteries and pay 0.04–0.10 €/kWh for surplus (table below). For their customers who own physical batteries, an optimiser that minimises imports at PVPC/indexed peak hours and exports little is directly bill-reducing. Repsol/Solar360 is the most "productised" (MediaMarkt channel, Telefónica IoT).

**Feníe Energía (ES).** Installers own the retailer. A white-label "Feníe Smart Battery" app sold through its ~2,000–2,400 installer agents is the fastest path to thousands of homes without individual installer contracts. Publicly announced plans for physical-battery control were not found.

**Lumio (ES).** It combines an indexed tariff (OMIE + 0.018 €/kWh), virtual energy communities priced at 90% of hourly OMIE, and installs Sungrow, Huawei and SolarEdge. Every kWh shifted by a battery is worth the full hourly spread. It is a small, fast decision-maker, which makes it a good design partner.

**Ingeteam / Circutor (ES).** These are Spanish OEMs whose apps and EMS do rule-based self-consumption only (as far as public sources show). An OEM licence would put the engine on every new hybrid inverter or controller they ship in Iberia.

- The Spanish residential market is shrinking while batteries rise. - 1KOMMA5° announced that Heartbeat AI "se lanzará próximamente en España" (Apr 2026). This puts competitive pressure on every Spanish installer without an optimiser.

- An academic benchmark from Comillas (2025 data) found optimal battery scheduling cut the bill by 17.1% versus the real inverter's own logic in an idealised run, but only by €5.37/year in the realistic version ([Comillas TFG](https://repositorio.comillas.edu/jspui/bitstream/11531/108554/2/TFG%20-%20Alvarez%20Solanas%2c%20Jose%20Antonio.pdf)). This is a caution: under simplified compensation the value per home can be small, so the pitch should lean on indexed/PVPC customers, EV charging and flexibility revenue (Holaluz claims ~€150/battery/year combined VPP value, company claim).
- No Iberian installer was found publicly stating that it *lacks* optimisation capability. The evidence of a gap is indirect: absence of features, Galp and EDP battery complaints, and Otovo selling an OEM HEMS.
