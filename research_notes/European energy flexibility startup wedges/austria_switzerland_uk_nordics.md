Scope note: research date 11 October 2026. ## Austria Q1: Energy communities (EEG/BEG/GEA) — numbers, grid-fee reductions, EDA process, software vendors, and who buys optimisation

### Takeaway
Austria has by far the densest energy-community ecosystem in Europe (5,590 EEGs at end-2025, ~500,000 metering points by Sept 2026) built on a real grid-fee discount (~57% local / ~28% regional for EEGs), a working 15-minute data pipeline (EDA) and a crowded field of billing/community-management vendors. The buyer of "optimisation" is unclear: communities themselves are volunteer associations with thin budgets; the realistic buyers are the billing/platform vendors (neoom, Exnaton, eFriends, 7Energy, enixi) and the new ElWG "Organisator" role — and storage inside EEGs is still legally awkward (a battery can be registered only as feeder or consumer, switched once a year).

### Cited Findings
- Count, end-2025 (Koordinationsstelle data as reproduced by a secondary site): 5,590 EEGs at end of December 2025, up from ~1,170 in Feb 2024 ("fast verfünffacht in nur zwei Jahren"); plus "rund 1.000" BEGs and "etwa 5.000" GEAs (undated). — [Elektronik-Zeit graphic citing Österreichische Koordinationsstelle, status end-2025](https://elektronik-zeit.de/grafik/energiegemeinschaften-oesterreich-wachstum/)
- Count, Sept 2026 (primary, Koordinationsstelle slides, Regionaltreffen 2026): "~ 500.000 Zählpunkte mit September 2026" and "+12.000 Modelle insgesamt" (all community model types). — [Regionaltreffen Energiegemeinschaften 2026 slides, energiegemeinschaften.gv.at](https://energiegemeinschaften.gv.at/wp-content/uploads/sites/19/2026/07/Regionaltreffen-Energiegemeinschaften-2026_Praesentationsfolien.pdf)
- One DSO example (Netz Niederösterreich, 10 Sept 2026): 102,787 participants/metering points in citizen-energy models: GEA 309 plants/1,479 participants; EEG 1,414 plants/83,220 participants; BEG 416 plants/18,088 participants. — [Regionaltreffen 2026 slides, Netz NÖ section](https://energiegemeinschaften.gv.at/wp-content/uploads/sites/19/2026/07/Regionaltreffen-Energiegemeinschaften-2026_Praesentationsfolien.pdf)
- A secondary summary attributes "3.868 Erneuerbare Energiegemeinschaften" (July 2025) to E-Control's EAG monitoring; this is ~1,700 below the end-2025 Koordinationsstelle figure and the two series should not be mixed. — reported in search summaries; underlying E-Control document not opened (see Gaps). Aggregator site claims "6.500+ Energiegemeinschaften österreichweit (GEA + EEG + BEG)" (non-official). — [erneuerbareenergiegemeinschaften.at](https://erneuerbareenergiegemeinschaften.at/)
- Grid-fee reduction in force for EEGs under ElWOG 2010 (still the applicable regime until the new SNE-VO): same low-voltage network (levels 6–7) "rund 57 % der Netznutzungsentgelte eingespart"; same medium-voltage level (levels 4–7) typically about 28%, up to 64% for levels 4–5 only; electricity tax and certain levies waived for allocated volumes; GEA (behind the meter) saves effectively 100% of grid-use and loss charges. BEGs have "keine strukturellen Netzentgeltreduktionen". — [CMS Austria legal update on gemeinsame Energienutzung under ElWG, 2025/26](https://cms.law/de/aut/legal-updates/gemeinsame-energienutzung-welche-moeglichkeiten-schafft-das-elwg)
- Concrete ct/kWh value of the discount (WKO Steiermark slide, 2026): "-28% auf NE 6/7 (bis zu -2,47 Cent/kWh)"; Elektrizitätsabgabe exemption for members "2026 reduziert auf -0,82 Cent/kWh". Secondary/slide source. — [WKO Steiermark Nachhaltigkeitstag 2026 slides](https://www.wko.at/stmk/kooperationen-fuer-die-zukunft-nachhaltigkeitstag26.pdf)
- ElWG timeline (rule in force): ElWG = BGBl. I Nr. 91/2025, promulgated 23 Dec 2025 ("Günstiger-Strom-Gesetz"); community/energy-sharing provisions (§ 65 Abs 1 Z 6, Abs 2, §§ 66–72) enter into force 1 Oct 2026. — [WKO ElWG overview](https://www.wko.at/energie/elektrizitaetswirtschaftsgesetz-elwg); [Koordinationsstelle, gemeinsame Energienutzung page](https://energiegemeinschaften.gv.at/gemeinsame-energienutzung/)
- Transition gap (Koordinationsstelle, July 2026 slides): the revised grid-tariff ordinance (SNE-VO) is expected 1 Jan 2027; "im Zeitraum Oktober bis Dezember 2026 keine vergünstigten Netzentgelte" for the new models (local/regional BEG, P2P, EVA); until end-2026 only local/regional EEG members get the reduced Netzarbeitspreis. New models can be implemented from 5 Oct 2026 only with metering points at the same grid operator; cross-DSO/nationwide and "Standortbereich" expected April 2027. — [Regionaltreffen 2026 slides](https://energiegemeinschaften.gv.at/wp-content/uploads/sites/19/2026/07/Regionaltreffen-Energiegemeinschaften-2026_Praesentationsfolien.pdf); [Koordinationsstelle page](https://energiegemeinschaften.gv.at/gemeinsame-energienutzung/)
- Future discount scope (lawyer slides, Niederhuber & Partner, 2026): "Künftig: alle gemEN bis zum Zonenlevel 5"; "Höhe der Reduktion von der Verordnung der ECA abhängig"; E-Abgabe and Erneuerbaren-Förderbeitrag exemption "gilt weiterhin nur für EEG". — [Regionaltreffen 2026 slides](https://energiegemeinschaften.gv.at/wp-content/uploads/sites/19/2026/07/Regionaltreffen-Energiegemeinschaften-2026_Praesentationsfolien.pdf)
- Nahebereich definitions under ElWG: Lokalbereich = LV network + transformer station, radius ~1 km (NE 6–7); Regionalbereich = MV network + substation, radius ~30 km (NE 4–7); Gebotszone = full grid fees. DSOs must provide Nahebereich info within 14 days (§ 70 Abs 6 Z 4 ElWG). Participation requires a smart meter (installed free on request) and an AT00 metering point ID. — [Koordinationsstelle page](https://energiegemeinschaften.gv.at/gemeinsame-energienutzung/)
- New "Organisator" role (ElWG § 68 Abs 2): service provider, not a market participant; tasks include registering/deregistering participants, allocation of energy volumes, communication with DSOs ("Anlegen eines Zugangs für die Nutzung des energiewirtschaftlichen Datenaustausches (EDA)"), "Unterstützung von Last- und Einspeisesteuerung durch Aggregierung (Z 3)", operating generation/storage up to 6 MW in own name; max one Organisator per community. Consent to pass on individual 15-min values (CCM process) is not covered by the Organisator mandate. — [Regionaltreffen 2026 slides (NHP section)](https://energiegemeinschaften.gv.at/wp-content/uploads/sites/19/2026/07/Regionaltreffen-Energiegemeinschaften-2026_Praesentationsfolien.pdf); [CMS](https://cms.law/de/aut/legal-updates/gemeinsame-energienutzung-welche-moeglichkeiten-schafft-das-elwg)
- Data access rules under ElWG: meter values to end customers and authorised third parties within 12 hours; suppliers, BRPs and aggregators receive data by next day 15:00; the aggregator "ist gemäß dem ElWG als eigener Marktteilnehmer qualifiziert". — [CMS](https://cms.law/de/aut/legal-updates/gemeinsame-energienutzung-welche-moeglichkeiten-schafft-das-elwg)
- EDA (Energiewirtschaftlicher Datenaustausch): operated by EDA GmbH; from 5 Oct 2026 registration for all market roles moves to www.ebutilities.at; communities registered before 5 Oct 2026 need no action; connection types are EDA Portal, e-mail connection, or KEP endpoint. — [eda.at](https://www.eda.at/)
- Smart-meter data quality (E-Control, report year 2024): 15-min data availability "über 99 % innerhalb der ersten 12 Stunden nach der Messung"; Netz NÖ lists "Umsetzung der Viertelstundenauslesung als Grundlage" and rising calculation/data-shipping load as 2026 challenges. — [E-Control Smart-Meter-Monitoringbericht 2025](https://www.e-control.at/documents/1785851/1811582/20251020-Monitoringbericht-SM-2025.pdf/4ce4b22f-8149-5162-770a-83f132511199?t=1763621630772); [Regionaltreffen 2026 slides](https://energiegemeinschaften.gv.at/wp-content/uploads/sites/19/2026/07/Regionaltreffen-Energiegemeinschaften-2026_Praesentationsfolien.pdf)
- Storage in EEGs (EGENT project, 2026 slides): a battery can take part in an EEG "aktuell nur als Einspeiser (S7) oder Verbraucher (S6)"; configuration change only once a year; S8 (charge from grid and feed in) counts any grid draw as grey power ("Kündigung durch OeMAG"); "Bezug aus EEG und Einspeisung in EEG weiterhin nicht möglich" because of the Regionalitätskriterium; proposal (not law) to exempt batteries up to e.g. 30 kW from the regionality criterion and allow 6-monthly switching. — [Regionaltreffen 2026 slides, EGENT section](https://energiegemeinschaften.gv.at/wp-content/uploads/sites/19/2026/07/Regionaltreffen-Energiegemeinschaften-2026_Praesentationsfolien.pdf)
- Vendors — Exnaton (ETH spin-off, white-label billing/energy-sharing platform): "more than 50 energy utilities" use it; API integration into ERP/billing; references include N-ERGIE and Thüga (German feasibility). — [Exnaton references](https://www.exnaton.ai/referenzen); [pv magazine profile, Sept 2024](https://www.pv-magazine.de/2024/09/27/start-up-des-monats-exnaton-entwickelt-abrechnungsysteme-fuer-dezentrale-energiesysteme/)
- Vendors — neoom KLUUB: founding, member management and billing platform; cost = energy price (~9–14 ct/kWh per neoom) + service fee. Company: 185 employees, €72.5m revenue; 88,000 storage systems installed at 15,000 customers (Nov 2025). — [neoom Kostenüberblick](https://wissen.neoom.com/kostenueberblick-egs); [energynewsmagazine, 4 Nov 2025](https://www.energynewsmagazine.at/2025/11/04/boom-bei-batteriespeichern/)
- Vendors — 7Energy: a BEG run as an association with automated prepaid billing (DAO/EURO-token smart contracts, AGPL); "50 aktive Netzgebiete", "95% der österreichischen Netzanschlüsse abgedeckt"; 2026 base price 8.95 ct/kWh, service fee 1.00 ct/kWh, one-off €12 per metering point membership; no forecasting/battery features mentioned. — [7energy.at](https://www.7energy.at/); [netidee project page](https://www.netidee.at/7energy)
- Vendors — eFriends: energy-sharing platform that is itself the supplier (eFriends Energy GmbH); 1,480 PV systems, 17 small hydro, 5 biogas, 50 PV storage systems listed; producers set 0–39.6 ct/kWh; 1.2 ct/kWh handling fee; 3 ct/kWh "Netzkostenvorteil" for trades within 10 km from 1 July 2025; forecast-based allocation for small consumers, second-level real-time metering mandatory above 10,000 kWh/yr. — [efriends.at](https://www.efriends.at/)
- Vendors — OurPower (cooperative SCE, Vienna, founded 2018): P2P marketplace with 400+ sellers; buyers from 15 ct/kWh incl. VAT; sellers up to 11 ct/kWh; no forecasting method described. — [ourpower.coop](https://www.ourpower.coop/)
- Vendors — enixi: platform for operational running of communities incl. energy-flow calculation and automated billing. — [enixi.io](https://enixi.io/)
- Koordinationsstelle notes "Hohe Anzahl an Anfragen ... Flexibilitäten" and that DSOs are moving from infrastructure operator to "aktiven Systemmanager"; the Regionaltreffen closing line: new rules "schaffen bessere Voraussetzungen für Flexibilität und Speicher". — [Regionaltreffen 2026 slides](https://energiegemeinschaften.gv.at/wp-content/uploads/sites/19/2026/07/Regionaltreffen-Energiegemeinschaften-2026_Praesentationsfolien.pdf)

### Inferences
- The economic lever that would make community-level forecasting/dispatch valuable (the local grid-fee discount applied to 15-minute netted volumes) is real, but the per-kWh prize is small (≤2.47 ct/kWh at NE 6/7, plus ~0.8 ct/kWh tax exemption). For a 100-member community sharing ~150 MWh/yr, better allocation forecasting is worth low four figures of euros per year — not a software budget. The vendor (neoom, Exnaton, eFriends) that bills hundreds of communities is the only entity for which an optimisation feature aggregates to a meaningful number.
- The ElWG "Organisator" explicitly includes "Last- und Einspeisesteuerung durch Aggregierung" — this is the legal hook that could let a software vendor sell dispatch (not just billing) to communities from Oct 2026, but the grid-fee regulation that monetises it slips to Jan 2027 and battery participation rules remain hostile (annual S6/S7 switch, no charge-from-community).
- Timing: Oct 2026–Apr 2027 is a regulatory transition with no new money flowing; vendors are busy with process changes (ebutilities registration, Nahbereichs-IDs). A new entrant pitching optimisation in this window will compete with compliance work for vendors' attention.

### Gaps
- No official E-Control document was opened for the July 2025 EEG count; the 3,868 figure comes from secondary summaries.
- The exact percentage/ct values of the 2027 SNE-VO discount for new models are not yet published (ordinance still draft as of July 2026).
- No source quantified software-vendor market shares, pricing per metering point, or whether any vendor buys external forecasting; no evidence found of any Austrian community operator purchasing optimisation.
- EDA technical process details (EC_REQ_ONL, fees) were not retrievable from eda.at's current page.

## Austria Q2: Dynamic tariffs, smart meters, home batteries/PV boom, feed-in and grid-connection limits, ElWG flexibility rules

### Takeaway
Austria's smart-meter rollout is effectively complete (96.9% / 6.53 million points at end-2024), seven suppliers offer hourly spot tariffs, and the home-battery stock (165,000 systems in 2024; 736 MWh of capacity) is large relative to population — but the policy direction in 2026 is toward curtailment and grid-friendliness (50% throttling of rooftop PV, storage mandatory for subsidies from 2027, peak capping from 20 kWp from 2027), which creates a near-term need for forecasting/dispatch that is still unmonetised.

### Cited Findings
- Smart meters (rule + data): IME-VO target 95% by end-2024; achieved 96.9% = 6,530,091 of 6,737,329 affected metering points at end-2024 (85.2% a year earlier); 90 of 117 DSOs met the target; 27 DSOs plan to reach it in 2025/2026; data availability >99% within 12 hours. — [E-Control Monitoringbericht Smart Meter 2025 (report year 2024)](https://www.e-control.at/documents/1785851/1811582/20251020-Monitoringbericht-SM-2025.pdf/4ce4b22f-8149-5162-770a-83f132511199?t=1763621630772)
- Dynamic tariff providers (June 2026 comparison): aWATTar HOURLY (EPEX Spot AT + 1.5 ct/kWh, no base fee), oekostrom oeko Spot+, smartENERGY smartCONTROL (+1.44 ct/kWh), Verbund HOURLY, Salzburg AG FlexSpot (EXAA), VKW Strom Dynamisch (quarter-hourly, +1.2 ct/kWh, Vorarlberg only), Energie AG dynamisch; all require a smart meter; prices exclude grid fees, taxes and levies; Tibber not available in Austria. — [smartmeter-portal.at provider comparison, June 2026](https://www.smartmeter-portal.at/dynamischer-stromtarif/anbieter-vergleich/)
- aWATTar launched "Sunny Spot 60 min", an hourly EPEX-indexed feed-in tariff (Nov 2025), requires remotely read smart meter; up to 50 kWp. — [pv magazine, 17 Nov 2025](https://www.pv-magazine.de/2025/11/17/marktorientierte-verguetung-awattar-startet-dynamischen-einspeisetarif-fuer-photovoltaik-in-oesterreich/); [stromliste.at aWATTar profile](https://stromliste.at/versorger/awattar)
- Home batteries: 165,000 systems installed in 2024 (+70,000 y/y) per Wirtschaftsministerium report; PV home storage capacity 2,200 MWh (Nov 2025 article); grids "zuletzt immer wieder an ihre Kapazitätsgrenze gelangt". — [energynewsmagazine, 4 Nov 2025](https://www.energynewsmagazine.at/2025/11/04/boom-bei-batteriespeichern/). A June 2026 article states installed storage capacity rose "von 212 auf 736 MWh" (unit per source; the two sources' MWh figures are inconsistent and are not reconciled here). — [Börse Express, 3 June 2026](https://www.boerse-express.com/news/articles/oesterreich-reformiert-solarfoerderung-batteriespeicher-ab-2027-pflicht-912535)
- Subsidy reform (proposal, June 2026): planned EAG amendment — investment grants only if a battery is commissioned at the same time; "Made in Europe" rule for inverters; rooftop plants must be throttleable to 50% of module capacity; OeMAG costs €60m in 2026 vs €15m prior year; storage subsidy €110m for 2025, current call raised to €70m; spot price hit −€500/MWh on 1 May 2026; draft "liegt den Koalitionspartnern zur Abstimmung vor". — [Börse Express, 3 June 2026](https://www.boerse-express.com/news/articles/oesterreich-reformiert-solarfoerderung-batteriespeicher-ab-2027-pflicht-912535)
- Storage demand study (PV Austria/APG/TU Graz/d-fine): battery need to grow eightfold to 8.7 GW by 2040, 5.1 GW by 2030. — [elektropraxis.at](https://elektropraxis.at/energiespeicherung/batteriespeicherbedarf-in-oesterreich/)
- ElWG grid-tariff/flexibility rules in force: Sommer-Nieder-Arbeitspreis (SNAP) on NE 7 from 1 April 2026 to 30 Sept 2026, −20% on the energy component 10:00–16:00, requires quarter-hourly smart-meter reads; optional "Regelbarer Tarif" on NE 3/4 with up to two 2-hour Sperrzeiten announced by 06:00 the day before, overrun billed at 10× capacity price. — [WKO ElWG overview](https://www.wko.at/energie/elektrizitaetswirtschaftsgesetz-elwg)
- ElWG from 2027: supply-infrastructure contribution on feed-in up to 0.05 ct/kWh; peak capping for new wind/PV plants from 20 kWp; large storage ≥1 MW exempt from consumption-side grid fees for 20 years if "system-supportive" (E-Control to define). "Flexibler Netzzugang" for capacity bottlenecks and grid-fee incentives for load/feed-in flexibility are in the law; no separate rule for flexibility providers. — [CMS](https://cms.law/de/aut/legal-updates/gemeinsame-energienutzung-welche-moeglichkeiten-schafft-das-elwg); [WKO NÖ ElWG info](https://www.wko.at/noe/transport-verkehr/spedition-logistik/elwg)
- ElWG names aggregators as new actors: "Flexibilitätsleistungen – etwa durch steuerbaren Verbrauch oder Speicher – können erstmals systematisch angeboten und durch Aggregatoren vermarktet werden". — [erneuerbare-energie.wien](https://www.erneuerbare-energie.wien/elektrizitaetswirtschaftsgesetz-elwg-impulse-fuer-eine-dezentrale-energiewelt/)
- Income-tax-free feed-in limited to plants ≤25 kWp connection capacity (since 2023). — [stromliste.at Einspeisetarife 2026](https://stromliste.at/umwelt/pv-anlagen/einspeisetarife)

### Inferences
- Data access is not the bottleneck in Austria (unlike Germany): quarter-hour values from a near-universal smart meter fleet reach suppliers/BRPs/aggregators by 15:00 next day under ElWG. The constraint is that only hourly-granularity retail tariffs exist (except VKW), so household-level dispatch value is capped by hourly spot spreads plus the small SNAP/EEG grid-fee signals.
- The 50% PV throttling and storage-mandate proposals, if enacted, create a mandatory need for inverter/battery control logic at ~every new residential PV system — but the buyer is the inverter/HEMS maker (Fronius, neoom, Huawei) not the homeowner.
- Austrian dynamic-tariff suppliers (aWATTar, smartENERGY, oekostrom) are candidates for buying residential load forecasting to reduce balancing cost (see Q3), but none publish customer numbers.

### Gaps
- No source gave customer numbers for any dynamic tariff provider or the share of smart meters with 15-min opt-in (IME vs IMS) — the E-Control report has a section but the extract did not surface the figure.
- No DSO-level grid-connection cap (e.g., Einspeiselimit in kW) was found in retrievable sources; only the 50%-throttling proposal.
- Status of the June 2026 EAG amendment (adopted or not) could not be confirmed as of 11 Oct 2026.

## Austria Q3: Balancing-market access via APG, aggregators and active players, imbalance settlement and whether BRPs buy forecasts

### Takeaway
APG's reserve markets are small (FCR ±75 MW in 2026, aFRR ±225 MW), have a 1 MW minimum bid and pooling is allowed — so residential aggregation is technically possible but the prize pool is tiny; the large commercial players (enspired, Wien Energie, Verbund) are focused on grid-scale BESS, and Next Kraftwerke is withdrawing from Austria. No evidence was found that Austrian BRPs buy third-party residential forecasts.

### Cited Findings
- FCR (rule in force): demand ±65 MW for 2025, ±75 MW for 2026; minimum bid ±1 MW in whole-MW steps; indivisible bids capped at ±25 MW; six 4-hour products per day; daily auction (gate opens D-7 10:30, closes D-1 08:00); capacity price only, pay-as-cleared; providers can pool facilities and choose which prequalified units deliver as long as aggregate volume/dynamics are met. — [APG primary control page](https://markt.apg.at/en/power-grid/balancing/primary-control/)
- aFRR (rule in force): demand ±225 MW from 13 Aug 2024 (±200 MW before); minimum bid 1 MW in 1 MW increments; capacity in six 4-hour blocks, daily tender (gate D-7 10:30 to D-1 09:00), capacity paid as-bid; energy bids in 15-min periods, gate T-25 min; elastic demand threshold ±1,000 EUR/MWh; AT–DE exchange limit under ALPACA 80 MW; APG in IGCC, PICASSO date not given. — [APG secondary control page](https://markt.apg.at/en/power-grid/balancing/secondary-control/)
- APG modalities (TC EBGL v1.4) allow APG to set a maximum size for indivisible bids. — [APG Modalitäten für Regelreserveanbieter v1.4](https://pb1-medien.apg.at/im/dl/pboxx-pixelboxx-20442/f,p/TC_EBGL_V1.4_clean.pdf)
- A third-party guide (2026) states aFRR minimum of 5 MW "currently (planned reduction to 1 MW)" — contradicted by APG's own page (1 MW); treat the APG figure as authoritative. — [greennrg guide](https://www.greennrg-gmbh.com/wissen/regelenergie-markt-srl-mrl-fcr-erklaerung) vs [APG](https://markt.apg.at/en/power-grid/balancing/secondary-control/)
- enspired (Vienna HQ, enspired Trading GmbH contracts Austrian customers): "Europe's leading battery optimizer", 4.3+ GWh BESS under management, 2.2+ GWh optimised live; markets AT, BE, DK, FR, DE, GR, IT, NL, PL, RO, ES, UK (Portugal "future"); named clients include Wien Energie, Uniper, Gore Street Capital, Obton; offers merchant/tolling/floor-price revenue models; German portfolio 1.12–1.28 cycles/day; €25m Series B in 2024. — [enspired-trading.com](https://www.enspired-trading.com/)
- Next Kraftwerke Austria: "entschieden haben, uns aus dem österreichischen Markt zurückzuziehen" (page asks for no new inquiries); group-wide 15,541 MW connected (Q4/2025), 14,375 units. — [next-kraftwerke.at](https://www.next-kraftwerke.at/)
- Imbalance settlement: APCS Power Clearing and Settlement AG is the imbalance settlement responsible / balance group coordinator for the APG control area, allocates balancing-energy costs to balance groups; price data at apcs.at/en/data-pricing and energymonitor.at. — [APCS](https://www.apcs.at/en)
- Austrian dynamic-tariff suppliers (Verbund, Energie AG, Salzburg AG, aWATTar) are listed in Q2; Verbund's flexibility-marketing page and Wien Energie's page returned 404 (see Gaps).

### Inferences
- Austria's total FCR+aFRR capacity market is roughly 300 MW symmetric; even at generous German-style prices this is a single-digit-million-euro-per-year pool, already served by hydro and grid-scale BESS. Residential aggregation for APG reserves is not a business; the exit of Next Kraftwerke from Austria is consistent with that.
- The one Austrian-headquartered company that plausibly buys ML forecasting (price, imbalance) at scale is enspired — but it sells optimisation itself and is more likely a competitor/acquirer than a customer.
- BRP forecast demand: Austrian suppliers with hourly spot tariffs face imbalance exposure on 15-min settlement but none published any RFP or purchase of third-party forecasting; this remains unproven.

### Gaps
- No APG/APCS price statistics (EUR/MW/h for FCR/aFRR, average imbalance price 2025) were retrievable; the APG pages contain no historic prices.
- Verbund, Wien Energie, Energie AG, Salzburg AG, EVN flexibility offerings could not be fetched (404s); Wien Energie is only confirmed as an enspired client.
- No source on whether any Austrian BRP purchases external forecasts.

## Austria Q4: Direktvermarkter and regional utilities as buyers; Austrian grants (FFG, aws) for foreign-incorporated companies

### Takeaway
Austrian grants are tied to a project location in Austria (aws explicitly; FFG page inaccessible), so a Munich GmbH would need an Austrian project site or subsidiary; the Direktvermarkter landscape is thinning (Next Kraftwerke out), leaving enspired and the big utilities as the realistic (but hard to reach) counterparties.

### Cited Findings
- aws Preseed – Innovative Solutions (rule): "The project location must be in Austria"; teams may apply before incorporation; established companies ≤6 months after register entry (Preseed) or ≤5 years (Seedfinancing); grant up to €89,000 (€100,000 with gender bonus); 80% funding rate (90% with bonus); micro/small enterprise per EU SME definition; no statement on foreign-incorporated applicants. — [aws Preseed Innovative Solutions](https://www.aws.at/en/aws-preseed-innovative-solutions/)
- aws Preseed/Seedfinancing overview: Seedfinancing for companies up to 5 years old; two lines (Innovative Solutions, Deep Tech). — [aws overview](https://www.aws.at/en/aws-preseed-seedfinancing/)
- Direktvermarktung in Austria runs under the EAG market-premium model; Next Kraftwerke offered this but is withdrawing. — [next-kraftwerke.at](https://www.next-kraftwerke.at/)
- enspired lists Wien Energie as a client; Axpo (Swiss) and others run cross-border pools, but no Austrian regional utility was found to publicly buy third-party forecasting. — [enspired-trading.com](https://www.enspired-trading.com/)

### Inferences
- A founder could become aws-eligible by placing the project (e.g., a pilot with an Austrian community platform) in Austria, but the cost of an Austrian entity is not justified by an €89k grant unless an Austrian customer exists first.

## Switzerland Q1: Market structure, Swissgrid products and pooling, Mantelerlass effects from 2025/26, and whether a German vendor can sell to Swiss utilities

### Takeaway
Switzerland is opening its balancing market to small assets (1 MW minimum for TRL/TRE from mid-2026, SRL/SRE mid-2027, Equigy crowd-balancing rollout 2027) and the Mantelerlass (in force 1 Jan 2026) creates LEGs, dynamic grid tariffs and minimum feed-in remuneration — but retail remains closed below 100 MWh/yr, the EU electricity agreement is "delayed" with no vote date, and the only residential flex aggregator of scale (tiko) shut its Swiss operations in January 2025. Selling to ~600 EVUs is a long, relationship-driven sale, not a product-led one.

### Cited Findings
- Partial liberalisation (rule in force): Parliament kept partial market opening for consumers above 100 MWh/year; ElCom FAQ distinguishes "marktberechtigte" and "nicht marktberechtigte" (basic-supply) customers. — [EVU Partners, 3 July 2024](https://www.evupartners.ch/de/blog/3rd-update-nach-dem-ja-zum-mantelerlass-geht-es-an-die-umsetzungen); [ElCom FAQ ES2050 ab Mantelerlass, version 16 June 2026](https://www.elcom.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/weRgj22ICsTE/053-00004_20260616_AN_FAQ%20ES2050%20ab%20Mantelerlass_DE.pdf)
- Mantelerlass: approved 9 June 2024 with 68.72%; ordinances adopted 19 Feb 2025; LEG and minimum-remuneration rules in force 1 Jan 2026. — [EVU Partners](https://www.evupartners.ch/de/blog/3rd-update-nach-dem-ja-zum-mantelerlass-geht-es-an-die-umsetzungen); [admin.ch press release 19 Feb 2025](https://www.admin.ch/de/nsb?id=104172)
- LEG rules (in force 1.1.2026, Art. 17d StromVG / Art. 19e–19h StromVV): participants in same grid area and same grid level; within one municipality; not above 36 kV; all participants need a smart meter; generation ≥5% of participants' connection capacity (storage not counted); storage may not sell more into the LEG per billing period than it draws from it (no arbitrage; "systematisches Laden aus dem Netz und Rückspeisen in die LEG ist untersagt"); shared volume computed on 15-min load profiles as min(feed-in, draw) and allocated pro rata; DSO must provide the computed "virtual" profiles free of charge; the grid-use discount does not apply to the metering tariff. — [ElCom FAQ, 16 June 2026](https://www.elcom.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/weRgj22ICsTE/053-00004_20260616_AN_FAQ%20ES2050%20ab%20Mantelerlass_DE.pdf)
- LEG grid-fee discount: the Feb 2024 draft ordinance set 30% (15% when several grid levels are used) and a 20% generation threshold (later cut to 5%). The final percentage could not be confirmed from the ElCom FAQ extract (see Gaps). — [EVU Partners, 3 July 2024](https://www.evupartners.ch/de/blog/3rd-update-nach-dem-ja-zum-mantelerlass-geht-es-an-die-umsetzungen)
- Minimum feed-in remuneration (rule, Art. 12 Abs. 1bis EnV, 1.1.2026): PV <30 kW: 6 Rp./kWh regardless of self-consumption; with self-consumption ≥30 kW: 6 Rp./kWh for the share up to 30 kW, 0 above (formula 180/kW); full-feed-in ≥30 kW: 6.2 Rp./kWh; otherwise quarterly-averaged reference market price. Draft had been 4.6 Rp./kWh. — [ElCom FAQ](https://www.elcom.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/weRgj22ICsTE/053-00004_20260616_AN_FAQ%20ES2050%20ab%20Mantelerlass_DE.pdf); [EVU Partners](https://www.evupartners.ch/de/blog/3rd-update-nach-dem-ja-zum-mantelerlass-geht-es-an-die-umsetzungen)
- Dynamic grid tariffs (rule): allowed on all levels if they incentivise grid-friendly behaviour; must be able to change at least hourly; tariffs must be set the day before; methodology published by end-August of prior year; basic-customer group (<50 MWh, LV, year-round) keeps static tariffs with ≥70% energy component; capacity-component share capped at 30/50% in the standard tariff; grid and metering tariffs separately published from 2026. Customers without smart meters cannot get dynamic tariffs. — [ElCom FAQ sections 5.1–5.9](https://www.elcom.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/weRgj22ICsTE/053-00004_20260616_AN_FAQ%20ES2050%20ab%20Mantelerlass_DE.pdf); [EVU Partners](https://www.evupartners.ch/de/blog/3rd-update-nach-dem-ja-zum-mantelerlass-geht-es-an-die-umsetzungen)
- DSO flexibility rights (rule, Art. 17c StromVG / Art. 19b–19d StromVV, 1.1.2026): DSOs may continue using "existing flexibility" (ripple-controlled boilers, heat-pump interruptions) if they inform holders in writing by post 1–31 Jan 2026; holders can opt out in writing within 30 days (or 3 months to year-end); any new use needs a flexibility contract (Art. 19b); guaranteed uses in case of imminent grid danger need no consent; draft capped guaranteed generation curtailment at 3% of annual energy with Parliament removing compensation. ElCom (June 2026) says DSOs may contact potential flexibility providers directly and may run geographically limited auctions, subject to efficiency principle. — [ElCom FAQ sections 8.1–8.7](https://www.elcom.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/weRgj22ICsTE/053-00004_20260616_AN_FAQ%20ES2050%20ab%20Mantelerlass_DE.pdf); [EVU Partners](https://www.evupartners.ch/de/blog/3rd-update-nach-dem-ja-zum-mantelerlass-geht-es-an-die-umsetzungen)
- Swissgrid Balancing Roadmap 2026–2030 (published Feb 2026): minimum bid reduced from 5 MW to 1 MW for TRL/TRE "Mitte 2026", for SRL/SRE "Mitte 2027" ("aus technischen Gründen ... nicht früher"); 96 gate closures/day for TRE from mid-2026; Equigy Crowd Balancing Platform pilot with full rollout "für 2027 vorgesehen"; harmonised prequalification; pooling concept (introduced 2013) under review to scale "für grosse Mengen dezentraler Flexibilität"; PRL demand 62 MW (2022) → 67 MW (2025) → 80 MW (2026); TSO-DSO coordination pilot; PV4Balancing pilot and a PV-specific TRE product; PICASSO/MARI participation "abhängig vom Stromabkommen" (Swissgrid observer; go-live preparations stopped in 2022); 15-min intraday auctions planned Q3 2026; Stromabkommen vote/entry into force "Zeitpunkt noch nicht definiert". — [Swissgrid Balancing Roadmap 2026–2030](https://www.swissgrid.ch/dam/jcr:60f5c050-5250-401b-9777-7253163eda20/balancing-roadmap-de.pdf)
- Swissgrid press (15 Jan 2026): new imbalance pricing mechanism from 1 Jan 2026; SRL activations −22% in first ten months of 2025 vs 2024, partly due to a new AI-based activation tool; storage hydro remains main supplier, solar/batteries/bidirectional EVs expected to grow. — [VSE/strom.ch press release, 15 Jan 2026](https://www.strom.ch/de/pressemitteilung/swissgrid-entwickelt-den-schweizer-regelenergiemarkt-weiter)
- Historical context: Swiss PRL market was ~117 MW with reserve remuneration "3,000 to 6,000 CHF per week per megawatt" (2014, historical). — [ee-news, 3 July 2014](https://www.ee-news.ch/de/article/29027/ekz-1-mw-batteriespeicher-ist-offiziell-am-markt-fuer-regelenergie)
- Datahub: the Mantelerlass creates a national data platform; "Der Umfang des Datahubs geht dabei deutlich weiter als die Branche teils möchte"; VSE leads operating standards. — [EVU Partners](https://www.evupartners.ch/de/blog/3rd-update-nach-dem-ja-zum-mantelerlass-geht-es-an-die-umsetzungen)
- Swiss PV: ~1.78 GW added in 2024, cumulative ~8.0 GW; consumption 57.5 TWh (2024). — [Wikipedia, Electricity sector in Switzerland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electricity_sector_in_Switzerland)

### Inferences
- The Swiss flexibility opportunity for a software vendor is DSO-side (Art. 17c contracts, local auctions ElCom explicitly permits, dynamic grid tariffs from 2026) and TSO-side via Equigy from 2027 — both are sales to utilities/DSOs, not to households, and both are in pilot phase in 2026.
- Non-EU status matters less for software than for market access: a German vendor can sell SaaS to a Swiss EVU, but cannot itself be a Swiss BSP without Swiss prequalification and a Swiss balance group, and the Equigy/pooling rules are still being redesigned.
- The LEG design (same municipality, no storage arbitrage, 5% rule, DSO computes the allocation) leaves less room for third-party optimisation than Austria's EEG model.

- Number of Swiss EVUs/DSOs (commonly cited ~600) could not be sourced (BFE/VSE pages 404).

## Nordics benchmark (NO/SE/FI): reserve markets for home batteries/EVs, the 2024–2026 FCR-D collapse, Finnish 15-min settlement and Datahub, durability of residential-flex revenue

### Takeaway
The Nordics show both the peak and the collapse of residential-flex economics: Swedish FCR-D prices went from 30–60 EUR/MWh (2022) to under €3 (Feb 2025) once prequalified capacity rose from <10 MW to ~600 MW against <550 MW demand; home-battery ancillary income (~€500/yr for 10 kW/10 kWh in Nov 2023–Oct 2024, CheckWatt) has since been replaced by a mix of mFRR, local flex and self-consumption; grid-scale index revenue fell >90% (SE: 1,700 → 100–200 kEUR/MW/yr; FI: 350–850 → 100–200 kEUR/MW/yr by May 2026). ### Inferences
- Durability lesson: a frequency-reserve market sized at a few hundred MW is exhausted by a few thousand home batteries or a handful of grid-scale BESS; the window between "aggregator launches" and "price collapses" was ~18 months in Sweden. Any residential-flex revenue model whose base case is FCR/FCR-D is structurally temporary.
- Where money survived: (a) mFRR/energy-market stacking for grid-scale assets (Flower ~€115k/MW/yr net, still 80%+ capacity-market driven), (b) C&I peak shaving (the largest line in the Swedish 200 kW example), (c) bundled "value" (Fever's €975/yr is earnings plus savings, not market revenue).
- The vendor models that persisted are B2B-to-utility (Fever, Emulate, CheckWatt as aggregator for installers), with the aggregator taking ~25% fee plus a safety margin (CheckWatt's own assumptions) — a useful calibration for what a German/Portuguese founder can charge: a quarter of a declining number.
- Finland's independent-aggregator regime (no supplier/BRP contract needed, central eSett settlement) is the regulatory end-state Germany's §41d/§14a path is slowly approaching; a founder can use Finnish rules as the "what good looks like" reference when assessing German market-access gaps.

- CheckWatt 2025/2026 revenue, number of connected batteries, and current per-battery earnings not published in fetched pages.

## What a Munich-based ML founder (with a Portuguese co-founder, no network) should take from Austria, Switzerland and the benchmarks

### Takeaway
Austria is the better second market (same language, EU rules, universal 15-min smart-meter data, 5,590 EEGs, hourly spot tariffs, a legal "Organisator/aggregator" role from Oct 2026) but its reserve markets are too small and its community economics too thin for optimisation to be a standalone product — the entry is as a feature supplier to community-billing platforms or dynamic-tariff suppliers. Switzerland is a 2027+ market (Equigy, 1 MW SRL in mid-2027, LEGs just started) sold utility-by-utility and should be deprioritised. The UK/Nordic benchmarks say: residential-flex revenue of £100–300 or €500/yr per asset is real but decays fast once reserve markets saturate, the software layer keeps roughly a quarter, and market access has to be rebuilt per country.

### Cited Findings
- Reserve market size comparison: Austria FCR ±75 MW (2026) and aFRR ±225 MW; Switzerland PRL 80 MW (2026) with ~1 GW each direction total; Sweden FCR-D <550 MW demand saturated by ~600 MW prequalified; Finland reserve need expected +134% in five years. — [APG FCR](https://markt.apg.at/en/power-grid/balancing/primary-control/); [APG aFRR](https://markt.apg.at/en/power-grid/balancing/secondary-control/); [Swissgrid Roadmap](https://www.swissgrid.ch/dam/jcr:60f5c050-5250-401b-9777-7253163eda20/balancing-roadmap-de.pdf); [Montel](https://montel.energy/resources/blog/battery-storage-market-insights-sweden); [Emaldo FI](https://emaldo.com/insights/emaldo-market-review-the-finnish-ancillary-services-market)
- Exits/retreats in the two target markets: tiko stopped Swiss operations 4 Jan 2025; Next Kraftwerke withdrawing from Austria (page live Oct 2026). — [tiko.ch](https://tiko.ch/); [next-kraftwerke.at](https://www.next-kraftwerke.at/)
- Residential take-rate reference: CheckWatt deducts "25% fees, a 15% safety margin, and a 60 SEK/month fixed fee" in its earnings calculations; Axle's take-rate "undisclosed". — [CheckWatt](https://checkwatt.com/en/services/); [Series A Stories](https://seriesastories.substack.com/p/in-home-energy-flexibility-the-biggest)
- Market-access non-portability: Axle's "market-code work is specific to Britain"; Fingrid's independent-aggregator regime required a 2018 national report, 2021 consultation, 2023 law change and a 2025 working group before aFRR opened in June 2025. — [Series A Stories](https://seriesastories.substack.com/p/in-home-energy-flexibility-the-biggest); [Fingrid 6/2026](https://www.fingrid.fi/contentassets/c59a6b9b7dbe4ea49200652ac3757962/62026-aggregation-info.pdf)
- Austrian data advantage: 96.9% smart-meter coverage, >99% 15-min data within 12 h, ElWG delivery to aggregators by 15:00 next day. — [E-Control SM report 2025](https://www.e-control.at/documents/1785851/1811582/20251020-Monitoringbericht-SM-2025.pdf/4ce4b22f-8149-5162-770a-83f132511199?t=1763621630772); [CMS](https://cms.law/de/aut/legal-updates/gemeinsame-energienutzung-welche-moeglichkeiten-schafft-das-elwg)
- Austrian grants require project location in Austria (aws). — [aws Preseed](https://www.aws.at/en/aws-preseed-innovative-solutions/)

### Inferences
- Concrete Austrian wedge: offer 15-min allocation/consumption/PV forecasting and battery-dispatch logic as an API to neoom KLUUB, Exnaton, eFriends or enixi (they hold the metering data and the customer relationship), timed for the SNE-VO discount extension (Jan 2027) and the new Organisator aggregation mandate; avoid pitching communities directly.
- Second Austrian wedge: imbalance-cost forecasting for the 7 hourly-tariff suppliers (aWATTar, smartENERGY, oekostrom, Verbund, Energie AG, Salzburg AG, VKW) — unproven demand, but these are the only Austrian BRPs with growing residential-profile risk.
- Benchmark-derived pricing discipline: budget the software take at ≤25% of a per-asset revenue that should be modelled to halve within two years of market entry (Swedish FCR-D path); a venture case needs ≥100k assets (Axle scale) or a utility/platform licence model (Fever/Emulate/Kaluza), not per-household subscriptions.
- Switzerland: watch Equigy full rollout (2027) and the SRL 1 MW threshold (mid-2027); until then the only Swiss buyers are Axpo/BKW/Alpiq-type pools with in-house teams.

### Gaps
- No source quantifies Austrian community-platform willingness to pay for forecasting; this is the key commercial unknown and should be tested by direct outreach (neoom, Exnaton, eFriends).
- No evidence of any Portuguese-linked advantage in these markets; Portugal appears only as enspired's "future" market.
