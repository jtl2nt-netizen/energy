## Italy — the deepest, most rules-complete market but with structural quirks (no negative prices, zonal transition pending)

### Takeaway
Italy has the most complete flexibility rulebook in the set (TIDE in force since 1 Jan 2025, 15-min ISP, MACSE storage auctions cleared, 6,000+ CER configurations with a 20-year GSE premium on shared energy), but the day-ahead price floor at zero removes the negative-price signal and the PUN→zonal retail transition still has no start date. For a software vendor the live pockets are CER optimisation/forecasting, UVAM/UVAZ aggregators adjusting to TIDE, and MACSE/merchant BESS optimisation in the South and islands.

### Cited Findings

**Price volatility and negative prices**
- Italian law/GME rules floor offers at zero; negative prices cannot form in Italy (opinion piece by GB Zorzoli, 3 Feb 2026) — [Rivista Energia](https://www.rivistaenergia.it/2026/02/prezzi-negativi-italia-zorzoli/)
- Ricardo/ENTSO-E analysis: Italy recorded 0 negative-price hours in Q1 2019–Q1 2026 (EU-27 Q1 2026: 1,223 negative hours vs 593 in Q1 2025) — [pv magazine, 8 May 2026](https://www.pv-magazine.com/2026/05/08/europes-negative-electricity-price-hours-double-in-q1-amid-renewables-surpluses-market-imbalances/)
- Q2 2026: South/Calabria/Sicily zones had 106 zero-price hours (4.9% of hours), Sardinia 74, North/Centre-North 13; record 9 consecutive zero-price hours on 12 May 2026; 215 hours ≤20 €/MWh in the South (159 of them 12:00–16:00); PUN Index June 2026 131 €/MWh, April/May 119 €/MWh; solar capture price Sardinia May 2026 64.8 €/MWh (58% of baseload) — [Italia Solare press release, 16 Jul 2026](https://www.italiasolare.eu/comunicati-stampa/mercato-elettrico-a-giugno-il-pun-supera-i-130-e-mwh-ma-al-sud-e-nelle-isole-il-prezzo-crolla-nelle-ore-di-sole/)
- 1 May 2026: PUN Index at 0 €/MWh for six consecutive hours nationally, first time at national level (secondary source) — [Sistemi Dinamici](https://www.sistemidinamici.eu/prezzi-negativi-energia-europa-italia/)
- PUN formally superseded by zonal prices from 1 Jan 2025, but a transitional mechanism reproduces PUN effects; full switch requires ≥12 months' notice and as of Feb 2026 no start date had been set — [Rivista Energia (Stagnaro), Feb 2026](https://www.rivistaenergia.it/2026/02/pun-prezzi-zonali-stagnaro/); [Rivista Energia (Zorzoli)](https://www.rivistaenergia.it/2026/02/prezzi-negativi-italia-zorzoli/)

**TIDE / dispatching / aggregation access**
- TIDE (ARERA 227/2025/R/eel, rev. 4 effective 5 Jun 2025) in force from 1 Jan 2025; transitional phase 1 Jan 2025–31 Jan 2026; consolidation phase from 1 Feb 2026; steady-state after 31 Dec 2026. Programs and balancing move from hourly to 15-minute ISP. BSP and BRP are not separated in the transitional phase; they can be split in the consolidation phase (i.e., the independent-aggregator split is a 2026 consolidation-phase feature). UVAM renamed UVAT (transitional) then UVAZ (zonal) at regime; aggregated participation extended to all technologies incl. non-programmable RES. New ultra-fast frequency reserve service — [Terna Lightbox "Il nuovo TIDE"](https://lightbox.terna.it/it/insight/tide-dispacciamento); [ARERA TIDE text](https://www.arera.it/fileadmin/allegati/docs/25/227-2025-R-eel-ALLEGATO_A.pdf)
- Existing UVAM dispatching contracts had been extended to 31 Jan 2026; existing UVAM may keep provincial aggregation perimeter as UVAT — [Terna ISP 15' webinar, Oct 2024](https://download.terna.it/terna/ISP15'_Webinar_20241025_8dcfce179cb8ea8.pdf)
- UVAM remuneration = fixed €/MW/yr (auctioned, cap 30,000 €/MW/yr in the pilot) + variable €/MWh on MSD activation; min 1 MW, 2-hour sustain. Historic auction outcomes (2021): ~23,184 €/MW/yr Area A, 24,059 Area B; afternoon product 5,938 €/MW/yr — HISTORICAL (2020–2022 sources) — [Qualenergia](https://www.qualenergia.it/articoli/aggregazione-la-nuova-frontiera-del-mercato-elettrico/); [ResearchGate (Italian DR regulation)](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/365233256_Demand_Response_in_the_Italian_regulation_and_first_results); [Staffetta, Apr 2021](https://www.staffettaonline.com/articolo.aspx?id=354236)
- Enel X claimed ~350 MW managed DR capacity in Italy (company claim, 2020, HISTORICAL) — [Enel X](https://www.enelx.com/it/it/storie/2020/07/demand-response-aziende-come-funziona)

**MACSE storage auction**
- First MACSE auction (30 Sep 2025): all 10 GWh assigned (Centro-Sud, Sud-Calabria, Sicily, Sardinia), offers >4× demand, weighted avg 12,959 €/MWh-yr vs reserve premium 37,000 €/MWh-yr; Centro Sud 14,566, Sud 12,146 €/MWh-yr; Li-ion, COD 2028; ~€1bn investment (Terna CEO). After one Enel Green Power project withdrew (32 MWh), 14 projects / 9,968 MWh — [pv magazine Italia, 1 Oct 2025](https://www.pv-magazine.it/2025/10/01/prima-asta-macse-assegnati-tutti-i-10-gwh-a-12-959-e-mwh-anno-medi/); [Il Sole 24 Ore](https://www.ilsole24ore.com/radiocor/nRC_01.10.2025_13.08_34410344); [Power-Solutions update, 10 Oct 2025](https://www.power-solutions.it/2025/10/10/%E2%9A%A0%EF%B8%8F-macse-2025-aggiornamento-sui-risultati-ritiro-di-un-progetto-e-nuova-pubblicazione-da-parte-di-terna/)
- Second MACSE auction reported for 24 Nov 2026, indicative need ~16 GWh per ARERA preliminary analysis (secondary/vendor source) — [MyEnergy blog](https://www.myenergy.it/blog/incentivi-e-agevolazioni/asta-macse-24-novembre-2026-regole-volumi-risultati/)

**CER (energy communities)**
- GSE, 31 Aug 2026: >4,800 configurations with active or finalising contracts, ~400 MW, >51,000 PODs (GSE CEO quote) — [ANSA, 7 Sep 2026](https://www.ansa.it/ansa2030/notizie/energia_energie/2026/09/07/sul-sito-del-gse-la-vetrina-delle-comunita-energetiche-rinnovabili_1035171a-d4e6-4aa1-bf8a-e6a1aa73e586.html)
- Same date, different count: 6,161 configurations operating, ~62,000 withdrawal points, 523.6 MW (likely includes all autoconsumo diffuso configurations, not only CER) — [Edilportale, 30 Sep 2026](https://www.edilportale.com/news/2026/09/risparmio-energetico/incentivi-cer-chiesta-la-proroga-dell-accesso-al-2030_112061_27.html). Earlier: 3,630 active configurations/302.9 MW at 30 Jun 2026 — [Energia in Città](https://www.energiaincitta.it/gse-lanciata-nuova-pagina-sulle-comunita-energetiche/); 1,805 configurations of which 597 CER at 31 Dec 2025 — [Solare Industriale](https://www.solareindustriale.it/blog-fotovoltaico/elenco-comunita-energetiche-rinnovabili). Definitions differ; treat the GSE/ANSA figure as the official one.
- Incentive: 20-year GSE premium tariff on shared energy, ~60–120 €/MWh with zonal uplifts (DM 414/2023); request window until 5 GW cap or 31 Dec 2027; ten associations asked on 30 Sep 2026 for extension to 2030 (proposal, not decided) — [Edilportale](https://www.edilportale.com/news/2026/09/risparmio-energetico/incentivi-cer-chiesta-la-proroga-dell-accesso-al-2030_112061_27.html); [Solare Industriale incentives](https://www.solareindustriale.it/incentivi/incentivi-cer-energia-condivisa)
- PNRR 40% capex grant: window closed 30 Nov 2025; 48,750 applications, 38,895 admitted, ~€1,031m awarded, 2,338 MW (GSE page, data labelled to 30 Sep 2026); facility raised to €1,041.5m; DM 127/2025 (in force 26 Jun 2025) removed the tariff reduction for PNRR recipients — [GSE Dati CER](https://www.gse.it/servizi-per-te/autoconsumo/gruppi-di-autoconsumatori-e-comunita-di-energia-rinnovabile/dati-e-informazioni-sulle-cer); [Edilportale](https://www.edilportale.com/news/2026/09/risparmio-energetico/incentivi-cer-chiesta-la-proroga-dell-accesso-al-2030_112061_27.html); [BibLus](https://biblus.acca.it/comunita-energetiche-cer-decreto-incentivi/)
- Vendor economics (Enpal, promotional, Aug 2026): consumer members earn ~15–50 €/yr, prosumers ~70–220 €/yr; management/GSE costs "a few euros" deducted from incentive; Enpal's own CER >2,000 members — [Enpal CER guide](https://www.enpal.com/it/fotovoltaico/guida-completa-comunita-energetiche-rinnovabili)

### Inferences
- Zero-price (not negative) hours concentrated in the South/islands in Q2 2026 mean the arbitrage signal exists for BESS/CER optimisation there, but household retail pass-through is muted while PUN-equivalent pricing persists.
- CER member payouts of tens of euros/year imply CER operators will not pay much per-POD for optimisation; the buyer is more plausibly the platform/ESCo operator (Enel X, Edison, Regalgrid, Sinergy, Evolvere, Sorgenia were named in the brief but could not be verified this pass) seeking shared-energy maximisation and GSE settlement accuracy.
- The 2026 BSP/BRP split under TIDE is the structural opening for independent aggregators; UVAZ product design is the thing to watch.

## Poland — biggest price signal in CEE, weak household pass-through, booming utility BESS

### Takeaway
Poland combines the deepest negative prices and widest day-ahead spreads in the group (avg daily spread ~€153/MWh in 2025, 310 negative hours) with mandatory dynamic tariffs that almost nobody uses (4,800 households, 0.03%) and a 1.66-million-microinstallation prosumer base now settled hourly. The money is currently in utility-scale BESS optimisation and aFRR/PICASSO; the household/cooperative layer is early.

### Cited Findings
- Negative prices: 310 negative-price hours in 2025 (annual), mean depth −15.75 €/MWh (deepest among compared markets), minimum −132.95 €/MWh; Q1 2026: 52 negative hours (vs 56 in Q1 2025) — [pv magazine / Ricardo, 8 May 2026](https://www.pv-magazine.com/2026/05/08/europes-negative-electricity-price-hours-double-in-q1-amid-renewables-surpluses-market-imbalances/). April 2025: 75 hours ≤0, record 223 GWh solar curtailed, ~200 MW BESS online — [Pexapark, 10 Jun 2025](https://pexapark.com/blog/prmc-polish-solar-capture-factors-hit-new-low-as-negative-prices-and-curtailment-surge/). April 2026: 87 negative hours, solar capture factor ~0.40 — [Pexapark 2026](https://pexapark.com/blog/european-solar-capture-factors-collapse-as-april-oversupply-triggers-wave-of-negative-prices/)
- Spreads/ancillary: avg daily DA spread ~€153/MWh in 2025 (~17% wider than Germany); intraday volume 6.7 TWh in 2025 (+170%); FCR 171–197 MW at ~€15–25/MW/h; aFRR up 400–500 MW at ~€35/MW/h, down ~€15–25/MW/h; PSE joined PICASSO 11 Jul 2025 — [Modo Energy, 20 Mar 2026](https://modoenergy.com/research/en/poland-pl-capacity-market-bess-cm-auction-merchant-revenue-stack-battery-march-2026)
- Capacity market: Dec 2025 auction cleared 5.1 GW physical BESS (~685 MW payable after 13.4% de-rating; de-rating fell 95%→60%→13.4%); 11–12 GW BESS scheduled by 2030; PSE transmission grid has zero free connection capacity at every substation for 2026 and 2031 — [Modo Energy, Mar 2026](https://modoenergy.com/research/en/poland-pl-capacity-market-bess-cm-auction-merchant-revenue-stack-battery-march-2026); [Modo pipeline](https://modoenergy.com/research/en/poland-battery-pipeline-buildout-queue-capacity-market-auctions-subsidy-grid-connection-queue). S&P: BESS 0.4 GW end-2025 → ~1.3 GW end-2026 — [S&P Global, May 2025](https://www.spglobal.com/energy/en/news-research/blog/energy-transition/052925-unlocking-polands-energy-storage-potential). OX2 FID on 50 MW/120 MWh with 17-yr CM contract (Jun 2026) — [ESS News](https://www.ess-news.com/2026/06/02/polands-capacity-market-supports-bess-investment-ox2-joins-with-120-mwh-project/)
- Retail pass-through: 4,800 households on dynamic tariffs at end-2025 (0.03% of individual customers; 0.05% of their consumption), up from 135 at start of 2025; 48% of households had remote-read meters at end-2025; PIE analysts cite low meter coverage, low awareness, no price caps in some offers, and flat network fees as reasons — [Portal Samorządowy, 3 Aug 2026](https://www.portalsamorzadowy.pl/gospodarka-komunalna/tanszy-prad-dla-nielicznych-z-taryf-dynamicznych-korzysta-zaledwie-0-03-proc-gospodarstw,669258.html). A conflicting ARE-based figure of ~40,000 dynamic contracts (all customers, ~20m PODs) was quoted by an industry representative — [money.pl](https://www.money.pl/gospodarka/taryfy-dynamiczne-korzysci-jedynie-dla-gospodarstw-domowych-o-wysokim-zuzyciu-7309409839200704a.html). CSIRE went live 2 Jul 2025 (24-hour switching); 15-minute settlement via CSIRE from 1 Jan 2026 — [sun-pro.pl (secondary)](https://sun-pro.pl/blog/taryfy-dynamiczne-energii-od-2026-jak-na-nich-zarobic-a-nie-stracic)
- Prosumers: >1.655 million microinstallations, >14.07 GW at end-May 2026; ~92,000 (1.06 GW) added in 2025; 5,800 (83 MW) in May 2026 — [PTPiREE](https://www.ptpiree.pl/energetyka-w-polsce/energetyka-w-liczbach/mikroinstalacje-w-polsce). Total PV 24.8 GW (ARE) / 25.5 GW (PSE) end-2025; prosumers <50 kW added just under 1 GW in 2025 — [pv magazine, 24 Feb 2026](https://www.pv-magazine.com/2026/02/24/poland-adds-3-6-gw-of-solar-in-2025/)
- Net-billing settlement prices (RCEm) for household installations went below zero for several hours in early April 2026 (Bloomberg, 6 Apr 2026, headline/snippet only) — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-06/polish-power-prices-turns-negative-for-some-as-renewables-boom). H1 2026 curtailment 1,174 GWh (+44.7% y/y); PSE curtailment so far only on MV/HV PV, household disconnections are voltage-driven — [Forum Energii monthly (snippet)](https://new.forum-energii.eu/en/monthly-magazine-1); [renewables.az](https://renewables.az/en/news/solar-curtailment-on-the-rise-in-poland)
- Mój Prąd 7.0: industry blogs describe a KPO-funded home-storage programme (~PLN 500m / ~€118m, up to PLN 16,000 / ~€3,800 per storage, start reported March 2026) but sources conflict on rules and start; no official NFOŚiGW confirmation retrieved — [smrinstalacje.pl](https://smrinstalacje.pl/blog/moj-prad-7-0-zasady-magazyny-energii-2026); [sunergo.pl](https://sunergo.pl/blog/czy-bedzie-moj-prad-7-0-co-juz-wiemy-o-programie-i-kiedy-moj-prad-2026-startuje/)

### Inferences
- Hourly RCEm settlement plus negative RCEm hours gives prosumers with batteries a real optimisation case; but with only ~0.03% on dynamic retail tariffs, the consumption side has no price signal yet, so the near-term software buyer is the BESS developer/trader and the retailer building dynamic offers, not the household.
- The de-rating collapse pushes BESS toward merchant/aFRR revenue stacks, which is exactly where forecasting and dispatch software is paid for.

## Czechia — energy-sharing early mover, aggregator rules landing Aug–Sep 2026, ČEPS prices falling

### Takeaway
Czechia has the most operational energy-sharing machinery in CEE (EDC live since Aug 2024; 11,679 sharing groups / 22,880 participants / 10.9 GWh by Apr 2025) and a balancing market where aggregators (Nano Energies) already win 12% of ČEPS long-term volume, but independent aggregation only becomes operable from Aug–Sep 2026, smart meters cover ~14% of consumers, and aFRR prices have roughly halved into 2026.

### Cited Findings
- Sharing stats: 31 Oct 2024 >4,300 groups/~12,000 participants; 30 Apr 2025 11,679 groups, 22,880 participants, 10.9 GWh cumulative (4.68 GWh in Apr 2025); apartment buildings ~5% of groups; energy communities a fraction of a percent; 2–3 weeks activation — [oEnergetice, six-months review](https://oenergetice.cz/en/quick-news/six-months-of-electricity-sharing-in-czechia-developments-statistics-and-future-directions). 20 energy communities established by Jan 2025; active-customer groups max 11 EANs; EC groups max 1,000; EC sharing limited to 3 neighbouring ORP regions or Prague until Jul 2026; static allocation keys for >50 EANs; hybrid key from 1 Jul 2026 — [Frank Bold, 24 Jan 2025](https://en.frankbold.org/news/electricity-sharing-in-czechia-a-strong-start-to-community-energy-and-a-promise-of-future-growth). EDC does not yet allocate dynamically; UKEN estimates up to 50% of shareable energy is lost — [EC Unlock your power – Czechia](https://energy.ec.europa.eu/topics/markets-and-consumers/energy-consumers-and-prosumers/unlock-your-power/unlock-your-power-czechia_en)
- Aggregation law: Lex RES III amendments entered into force Aug and Nov 2025; active customers may contract independent aggregators without supplier consent; aFRR/mFRR participation via independent aggregator; EDC functionality for storage/flexibility/aggregation to be expanded by 1 Aug 2026, data exchange fully operational by 1 Dec 2027; consultation respondents say implementing rules are missing so DR in balancing markets is expected only by 1 Aug 2026 and in wholesale by 1 Dec 2027 — [EC Opinion C(2026) 1619, 12 Mar 2026](https://webgate.ec.europa.eu/circabc-ewpp/d/d/workspace/SpacesStore/12a39a1e-e459-4e0c-89ef-cc6796e11158/download). Independent aggregators may operate from Sep 2026; new tariff structure Jan 2027 — [oEnergetice, 5 Oct 2026](https://oenergetice.cz/en/energy-storage/the-czech-flexibility-market-is-changing-saf-conference-2026-will-address-questions-that-will-determine-how-it-works)
- Smart meters: 365,000 installed, target 1.867m by 2030; roll-out focused on >6 MWh/yr consumers, complete Q2 2027 = 14.18% of consumers / 60.86% of consumption; <6 MWh consumers get a free meter if needed for sharing/flexibility; all meters since 1 Jan 2025 are 15-min capable; CBA for <6 MWh by 31 Dec 2028; consultation respondent: 3% of households had a smart meter (SmartEN 2024). Dynamic price contracts legally available (Art. 11) — [EC Opinion, Mar 2026](https://webgate.ec.europa.eu/circabc-ewpp/d/d/workspace/SpacesStore/12a39a1e-e459-4e0c-89ef-cc6796e11158/download)
- Balancing market: aFRR+ long-term avg 20.8 €/MW/h (2025) → 10.8 (2026; H2 2026 ~7.0); aFRR+ DA 16.3 (2025) → 6.9 (to May 2026); ČEPS bought no aFRR−/mFRR− capacity for H2 2026; Nano Energies (company claim) secured 12% of long-term volume, first among aggregators, >280 MW; top providers ČEZ, Teplárna Kladno, EIFlexi — [oEnergetice, 10 Jun 2026](https://oenergetice.cz/en/czech-energy-sector/balancing-services-market-is-changing-as-ceps-auctions-show-rise-of-aggregators-and-pressure-on-flexibility-prices)
- BESS: Second Foundation to commission 307 MW in 2026 incl. 200 MW/400 MWh Tušimice (first direct ČEPS EHV connection), optimised in-house via acquired Nano Energies — [Energy-Storage.News](https://www.energy-storage.news/czech-republic-trading-firm-second-foundation-to-commission-307mw-of-bess-in-2026-local-integrator-used/); [pv Europe](https://www.pveurope.eu/solar-storage/czech-firm-lines-307-mw-storage-including-direct-transmission). Resource adequacy: LOLE 8.4 h identified for 2026; no capacity mechanism — [EC Opinion](https://webgate.ec.europa.eu/circabc-ewpp/d/d/workspace/SpacesStore/12a39a1e-e459-4e0c-89ef-cc6796e11158/download)
- Ecosystem: SAF 2026 conference (20 Oct 2026) partners include Nano Energies, aggregaat, E.ON, Emaldo, Enery, GAZ Energy, MND, Photomate, Solar Global, Sungrow, Tesla Energy Group — [oEnergetice](https://oenergetice.cz/en/energy-storage/the-czech-flexibility-market-is-changing-saf-conference-2026-will-address-questions-that-will-determine-how-it-works)

### Inferences
- Falling aFRR prices and ČEPS dropping downward capacity procurement compress the aggregator margin; the competitive edge shifts to forecasting and DA/intraday optimisation, which favours a software vendor over a new aggregator.
- The Aug 2026 EDC flexibility expansion plus the 2027 tariff reform is the window where sharing groups and communities will first need dispatch optimisation (dynamic allocation + batteries).

### Gaps
- Dynamic-tariff uptake numbers, household PV/battery counts, NZÚ subsidy terms: not retrieved. Havel & Partners Lex RES III note was rate-limited.

## Hungary — regulated household prices, gross metering since Oct 2023, storage subsidies; weakest signal to households

### Takeaway
Hungary keeps households on capped regulated tariffs (maintained into 2026), moved new rooftop PV to gross metering with a feed-in price, and froze feed-in tariffs at 2024 levels to 2030; wholesale volatility is real (−41 to >640 €/MWh in one day in 2025) but is deliberately not passed to households. Household batteries (late-2025 subsidy, tens of thousands of applications) are the one growing small-asset pocket; aggregator and energy-community frameworks could not be verified this pass.

### Cited Findings
- Solar ~8.4 GW end-2025 (+1.1 GW in 2025); solar ~27% of electricity in 2025; June 2025 solar briefly covered virtually all demand — [Legal 500 Hungary Renewable Energy guide 2026](https://legal500.com/guides/chapter/hungary-renewable-energy/?export-pdf=)
- Mandatory feed-in tariff regime amended end-Jan 2025, freezing tariffs at 2024 levels to end of decade; settlement moved to 15-minute units; TSO-instructed curtailment excluded from settlement penalties; new household solar shifted from net to gross metering; grid-communicating inverters required; residential storage programme launched end-2025 (fixed subsidy towards 10 kW home batteries) attracted tens of thousands of applications within days; co-location framework for storage ≥0.5 MW from 1 Jan 2025 — [Legal 500 2026](https://legal500.com/guides/chapter/hungary-renewable-energy/?export-pdf=)
- Utility storage: operational ~60–70 MW early 2026, ~550 MW licensed expected by mid-2026, target 1 GW by 2030; end-2024 tender ~€158m for ~440 MW; further grant+10-yr CfD scheme for 600 MW; MAVIR commissioned its own grid battery — [Legal 500 2026](https://legal500.com/guides/chapter/hungary-renewable-energy/?export-pdf=)
- Household regulated-price regime (rezsicsökkentés) maintained and extended into 2026; divergence between market and regulated price absorbed centrally — [Legal 500 2026](https://legal500.com/guides/chapter/hungary-renewable-energy/?export-pdf=)
- Metering regimes: systems commissioned before 31 Oct 2023 keep net metering until 31 Dec 2035; newer systems on gross scheme with regulated feed-in price and full retail price on consumption (Chambers 2025 guide, now offline; per search snippet) — [Chambers (snippet)](https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/renewable-energy-2025/hungary). MEKH 2024 presentation: feed-in restrictions lifted in most of Hungary from Jan 2024 — [ERRA/MEKH, Mar 2024](https://erranet.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Krajcs_MEKH_Hungarian_Case_Joint_Bratislava_Mar2024.pdf)
- Volatility: by end-Aug 2025 Hungary had more zero/negative-price hours than all of 2024; intraday swung −41 to >640 €/MWh within one day; solar record 6,361 MW on 1 Jul 2025 (>90% of demand); E.ON Soroksár 2.5 MW/5.5 MWh battery — [MET3R blog (undated, 2025)](https://met3r.com/blog/hungarys-electricity-market-faces-record-negative-prices/)
- Prosumers HISTORICAL: 88,112 households in feed-in scheme, 719 MW (2020, MEKH); target 200,000 by 2030 — [Serbia Energy, May 2021](https://serbia-energy.eu/hungary-prosumers-in-feed-in-tariff-scheme-increased-by-49/)

## Bulgaria — extreme wholesale volatility and a world-leading battery boom, but households still regulated and aggregation unusable at scale

Bulgaria has arguably the most violent day-ahead in Europe (−€100/MWh on 25 Apr 2026, €282/MWh in Jan 2026) and went from ~0 to ~8.6 GWh of BESS by May 2026 via the EU-funded RESTORE scheme, yet households remain on regulated tariffs (liberalisation postponed indefinitely in May 2025), smart-meter coverage is 15–75% by DSO with no common standard, and the aggregator/energy-community framework is "legally enabled but operationally underdeveloped" (six communities total).

### Cited Findings

## Greece — net-billing since Sept 2024, sharpest negative-price jump in Q1 2026, 1,700+ communities but a troubled sector

### Takeaway
Greece switched new prosumers to 15-min net-billing (Sept 2024) with a 20-year €65.74/MWh export option, saw negative-price hours jump from 0 to 138 in Q1 2026, and is rolling out ~1m smart meters in 2026; but energy communities (1,742–1,747 registered, ~1.1–1.47 GW) are being sold to funds, half of requested capacity was cancelled for lack of grid, and a 24 Jun 2026 rule strips premium payments for all negative-price hours.

## France — most mature demand-response market, regulated-tariff-shaped household flexibility, reformed capacity mechanism from winter 2026–27

### Takeaway
France offers the largest certified demand-response base (3.8–4.5 GW certified per RTE 2024; Voltalis 200,000+ homes), a redesigned centralised capacity mechanism (EC-approved 22 Dec 2025, RTE sole buyer, 4.7 GW reserved for DR/storage in the 2026–27 auction, caps €72/kW and €15/kW), and an Enedis/CRE off-peak reform moving 11m households to afternoon heures creuses (Nov 2025–Nov 2027). Household flexibility is shaped by regulated tariff structures (Tempo/HC) more than by spot prices, and the incumbents (Voltalis, Energy Pool, Enel X, Agregio) are entrenched.

- Effacement/NEBEF: RTE certified ~3.8–4.5 GW effacement (2024); PPE 2024–28 target 6.5 GW; PPE3 draft 10 GW by 2030; Voltalis 200,000+ homes (2023), ~500 MW (another figure 721 MW, inconsistent); Energy Pool 2,500 MW; Agregio 6 GW (undated); Tiko Services ceased French activity end-2025; ~900,000 Tempo and ~300,000 EJP meters (undated) — [Demongeot blog (secondary, Jan 2026)](https://www.demongeot.biz/blog/effacement-diffus-france.html). - Negative prices: France not covered in the Ricardo Q1 table — [pv magazine](https://www.pv-magazine.com/2026/05/08/europes-negative-electricity-price-hours-double-in-q1-amid-renewables-surpluses-market-imbalances/). RTE bilan 2025 page returned 404.

### Inferences
- A 5.5 €c/kWh HP/HC spread plus the new afternoon window creates a scheduling problem for 11m households (heat pumps, EVs, water heaters) that is retailer/OEM-driven rather than spot-driven; the software buyer is the retailer or box vendor, not the household.
